5. Simulate

When Simulate is ticked, synthetic plucked tones are fed into the detector instead of the mic. Pattern tokens: slot:midi (pluck), slot:midi~ (hammer-on/pull-off, no new attack), slot:- (muted knock). Note names work too (0:A3).
